# RESEARCH NOTES

## The German train (Scene 1 cut)
**Decided: BR 52 Kriegslok, black, flags on the front.**
Not in the earlier notes. Findings:
- German classes documented running in Denmark 1940–45: BR 17, 38, 50, 52, 55, 56, 57, 89, 93, 99 (Arbeitskreis Lokgeschichte, via signalposten.dk).
- **BR 38.10** (Prussian P8): confirmed in Jutland from 1940, hauling the "SF" fast trains for naval personnel, Flensburg up through Jutland, until 1944. Most likely German engine actually crossing Langå in 1943.
- **BR 52 Kriegslok**: the iconic black war locomotive, built from Sept 1942, did run in Denmark. The stronger film image, plausible but less certain on this line.
- Caveat: most German military transports in Jutland were hauled by DSB engines (litra E, R, P) with Danish crews. A German-crewed black engine is the exception, which is why it reads as the enemy.
- Sources: signalposten.dk forum thread 2129; jernbanen.dk. Book recommended there: "Roer på skinner".
